# Quality, safety, privacy, fairness, and fraud

Trust is a system-wide constraint: an economically attractive ad can still be harmful, deceptive, discriminatory, privacy-invasive, or invalid.

## Ad quality and user experience

- [The Good, the Bad, and the Random: An Eye-Tracking Study of Ad Quality in Web Search](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/the-good-the-bad-and-the-random-an-eye-tracking-study-of-ad-quality-in-web-search/) — Buscher et al., *SIGIR 2010*. Measures how ad quality changes attention and search behavior.
- [Focus on the Long-Term: It's Better for Users and Business](https://research.google/pubs/focus-on-the-long-term-its-better-for-users-and-business/) — Studies how prior relevance and landing-page experience affect future attention to ads.
- [Sponsored Search Ad Selection by Keyword Structure Analysis](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/?p=164551) — *ECIR 2013*. Uses keyword structure when selecting relevant ads.

## Fairness and discrimination

- [Toward Controlling Discrimination in Online Ad Auctions](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/mehrotra19a.html) — Mehrotra et al., *ICML 2019*. Mechanisms for constraining discriminatory delivery outcomes.
- [Discrimination through Optimization: How Facebook's Ad Delivery Can Lead to Skewed Outcomes](https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.02095) — Ali et al., 2019. Shows that delivery optimization can skew audiences even without discriminatory targeting.
- [Measuring and Mitigating Bias and Harm in Personalized Advertising](https://doi.org/10.1145/3460231.3473895) — *RecSys 2021*. Measurement and mitigation methods for harms in personalized ads.
- [Fairness in Online Ad Delivery](https://facctconference.org/static/papers24/facct24-95.pdf) — *FAccT 2024*. Contemporary analysis of fairness in delivery systems. `recent`

## Fraud and invalid traffic

- [Exploring the Dynamics of Search Advertiser Fraud](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/imc17-adfraud.pdf) — *IMC 2017*. Empirical study of fraudulent search advertisers and ecosystem dynamics.
- [Clicktok: Click Fraud Detection using Traffic Analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.00733) — 2019. Detects click fraud through temporal traffic patterns.

## Privacy

- [A Practical Application of Differential Privacy to Personalized Online Advertising](https://eprint.iacr.org/2011/152.pdf) — 2011. Applies differential privacy to ad personalization.

Also study policy enforcement, advertiser verification, malware and landing-page detection, brand safety, sensitive targeting, invalid clicks, consent, data minimization, and privacy-preserving measurement.
