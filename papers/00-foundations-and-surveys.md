# Foundations and surveys

Start here for the vocabulary, actors, objectives, and end-to-end structure of computational advertising.

## Core reading

- [Introduction to Computational Advertising](https://aclanthology.org/P08-5001.pdf) — Broder, *ACL 2008*. A compact map of sponsored search, contextual advertising, and behavioral targeting. `seminal` `survey`
- [Algorithmic Methods for Sponsored Search Advertising](https://research.google/pubs/algorithmic-methods-for-sponsored-search-advertising/) — Lahaie et al., 2007. A survey of mechanisms and algorithms behind sponsored search. `survey`
- [Online Matching and Ad Allocation](https://research.google/pubs/online-matching-and-ad-allocation/) — Mehta, *Foundations and Trends in Theoretical Computer Science 2013*. A rigorous treatment of online matching and budgeted allocation. `survey` `theory`
- [Internet Advertising and the Generalized Second-Price Auction](https://pubs.aeaweb.org/doi/10.1257/aer.97.1.242) — Edelman, Ostrovsky, and Schwarz, *AER 2007*. The canonical economic analysis of sponsored-search auctions. `seminal`
- [Counterfactual Reasoning and Learning Systems: The Example of Computational Advertising](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v14/bottou13a.html) — Bottou et al., *JMLR 2013*. Connects prediction, intervention, logged data, and policy feedback loops. `seminal`

## Useful companion topics

- Information retrieval: candidate generation, scoring, and evaluation.
- Recommender systems: embeddings, two-stage architectures, and feedback bias.
- Economics: auction theory, incentives, welfare, and revenue.
- Operations research: constrained optimization, control, forecasting, and allocation.
- Statistics: experiments, causal inference, attribution, and uncertainty.
