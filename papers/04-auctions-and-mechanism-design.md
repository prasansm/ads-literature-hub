# Auctions and mechanism design

Auction design converts predicted value and bids into allocation, placement, and price while considering incentives, user experience, revenue, and welfare.

## Foundations

- [Internet Advertising and the Generalized Second-Price Auction](https://pubs.aeaweb.org/doi/10.1257/aer.97.1.242) — Edelman, Ostrovsky, and Schwarz, *AER 2007*. Canonical analysis of GSP equilibria. `seminal`
- [Position Auctions](https://people.ischool.berkeley.edu/~hal/Papers/2006/position.pdf) — Varian, *International Journal of Industrial Organization 2007*. Equilibria and empirical behavior in position auctions. `seminal`
- [AdWords and Generalized Online Matching](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~vazirani/pubs/adwords.pdf) — Mehta et al., *JACM 2007*. Online allocation under budgets and the `1 - 1/e` result. `seminal` `theory`
- [Sponsored Search Auctions with Markovian Users](https://arxiv.org/abs/0805.0766) — Aggarwal et al., 2008. Models examination, abandonment, and interactions among ads on a page.
- [Online Matching and Ad Allocation](https://research.google/pubs/online-matching-and-ad-allocation/) — Mehta, *Foundations and Trends 2013*. A unified theoretical reference for online allocation. `survey`

## New interfaces and objectives

- [Ad Auctions for LLMs via Retrieval Augmented Generation](https://research.google/pubs/ad-auctions-for-llms-via-retrieval-augmented-generation/) — *NeurIPS 2024*. Explores auction design when ads are retrieved and integrated into generated responses. `recent`
- [Toward Controlling Discrimination in Online Ad Auctions](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/mehrotra19a.html) — Mehrotra et al., *ICML 2019*. Adds delivery constraints intended to control discriminatory outcomes.

## Concepts worth mastering

GSP, VCG, first-price auctions, reserve prices, quality-adjusted bids, incentive compatibility, welfare, revenue, price of anarchy, and budget-constrained allocation.
